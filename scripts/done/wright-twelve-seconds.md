On a cold morning in December nineteen oh three, on a beach in North Carolina, Orville Wright flew for twelve seconds. He covered a hundred and twenty feet. Five people were there to watch. The brothers were bicycle makers from Ohio, with no college degree and no money behind them but their own shop. They flew four times that day, and the longest flight lasted fifty-nine seconds. Then a gust of wind flipped the machine over and wrecked it. Twelve seconds does not sound like much. But before that morning the number was zero, and that is the hardest gap there is. What is your twelve seconds, the small rough first version that finally leaves the ground?
