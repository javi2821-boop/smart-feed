title: What is it like to be a bat?
character: young-woman

source: Thomas Nagel, "What Is It Like to Be a Bat?", 1974

In nineteen seventy-four the philosopher Thomas Nagel asked a question that sounds like a joke. What is it like to be a bat? A bat finds its way by calling out and listening to the echoes. We can study its brain down to the last cell. We can even imagine hanging upside down in the dark. But Nagel argued that this only tells us what it would be like for us to behave like a bat. It does not tell us what it is like for the bat. There seems to be a fact about its inner life that no outside description reaches. And if that is true of a bat, how much of the person next to you can you really know?
