title: The experience machine
character: scientist
background: workshop
source: Robert Nozick, Anarchy, State, and Utopia (1974)
---
In nineteen seventy-four, the philosopher Robert Nozick imagined a machine. You float in a tank, wires on your head, and the machine feeds you any life you choose. You write the great novel. You are loved. You win. From the inside it all feels completely real, and you never remember plugging in. Nozick noticed that most people hesitate. They sense that feeling good is not the same as doing something, or being someone. But nobody can say exactly what would be missing. So here is his question, and it is still open. If the machine were ready tonight, would you plug in for the rest of your life?
