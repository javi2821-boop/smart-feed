Peter Thiel writes that he likes to ask one question in job interviews, and that most people cannot answer it. What important truth do very few people agree with you on? It sounds easy. It is not. Most answers are things nearly everyone already believes, said with a brave face. A real answer costs something, because by definition the room is against you. Thiel's point for builders is that a good company rests on one of these, a true thing that most people still think is false. Forget companies for a moment and just try it honestly. What important truth do very few people agree with you on?
