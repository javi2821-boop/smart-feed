title: The letter Lincoln never sent
character: old-man
background: study
source: Lincoln's unsent letter to General Meade, July 1863 (paraphrased)
---
In July of eighteen sixty-three, Abraham Lincoln was furious. His general, George Meade, had won at Gettysburg and then let the beaten army slip away across the river. Lincoln sat down and wrote Meade a hard letter, telling him how deeply the missed chance distressed him. Then he folded it, put it in an envelope, and noted on it that it was never sent and never signed. It was found among his papers after he died. He let himself say everything, and he let Meade read none of it. Anger wants a reader right away. Lincoln gave his a drawer instead. What could you write tonight, and not send?
