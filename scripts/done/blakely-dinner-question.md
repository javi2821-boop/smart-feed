type: story
title: What did you fail at this week?
character: dad
background: study
source: Sara Blakely, in interviews about her father (paraphrased)
---
When Sara Blakely was growing up, her father asked the same question at the dinner table. What did you fail at this week? If she and her brother had nothing to report, he was disappointed. If she had tried out for something and been terrible, he would high-five her. She has said it changed what the word meant to her. Failure stopped being a bad result. It became not trying. Years later she heard no from one hosiery mill after another, while she sold fax machines door to door, and she kept going until she had founded Spanx. One question, asked every week, had changed what she was afraid of. What question gets asked at your table?
