title: Be still
character: mother
background: church
source: Psalm 46:10 (KJV)
---
Psalm forty-six describes the earth giving way and the mountains carried into the sea. Nations rage. Kingdoms are moved. And then, near the end, God speaks one short line. Be still, and know that I am God. That is the tenth verse, in the King James. Notice where it comes. Not after the trouble has passed. In the middle of it. The stillness is not a reward for having everything under control. It is an admission that you never did, and that someone else does. Stopping can feel like the one thing you cannot afford today. The psalm says it comes first. Where could you be still for two minutes, before the next thing?
