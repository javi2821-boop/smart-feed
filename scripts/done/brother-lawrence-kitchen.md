title: The monk in the kitchen
character: monk

source: Brother Lawrence, The Practice of the Presence of God (paraphrased)
---
In the sixteen hundreds, a former soldier named Nicolas Herman joined a monastery in Paris. He was not a scholar, so they put him in the kitchen. He took the name Brother Lawrence, and for years he scrubbed pots and cooked for the whole community. By his own account he disliked kitchen work. So he made a private decision. He would do each small task as if it were for God, and talk with God while he did it. Visitors later said he was as much at peace among the clattering pans as other monks were on their knees in chapel. He did not wait for holier work. He changed what the work was. What is your kitchen?
