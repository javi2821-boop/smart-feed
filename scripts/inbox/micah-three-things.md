The prophet Micah pictures someone asking what God really wants. Thousands of rams? Rivers of oil? Something huge and costly? The answer, in the King James, is Micah six, verse eight. He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth the Lord require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God? Three things, and none of them is impressive. Do what is right. Love mercy, not just show it when you must. And walk, which means daily and slowly, without thinking much of yourself. We keep offering something big. He keeps asking for something near. Which of the three is yours today?
