---
type: scripture
title: One day's strength
character: pastor
background: church
source: Matthew 6:34 (KJV)
---
Jesus said this near the end of the Sermon on the Mount. Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof. That is Matthew six, thirty-four, in the King James. Notice what he does not say. He does not say tomorrow will be easy. He says tomorrow has its own trouble, and today has its own too, and you were given strength for one of them. Worry is trying to carry two days on one day's strength. So what is actually in front of you today? Do that.
