---
type: lesson
title: Start with something small that works
character: worker
background: workshop
source: John Gall, Systemantics, 1975 (paraphrased)
---
John Gall studied why big systems fail, and he arrived at a rule that builders still repeat. He observed that a complex system that works turns out to have grown from a simple system that worked. And a complex system designed from scratch, he said, does not work, and cannot be patched into working. You have to start over with something small that runs. It is humbling, because the grand plan is the fun part. But it tells you what to build this week. Not the whole machine. The smallest piece that does one real job for one real person. Get that running, and let everything else grow out of it.
