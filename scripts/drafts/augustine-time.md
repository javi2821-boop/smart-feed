---
type: question
title: What is time?
character: monk
background: church
source: Augustine, Confessions, Book XI (paraphrased)
---
Augustine of Hippo was a bishop and one of the sharpest minds of the ancient world, and one small word defeated him. Time. In his Confessions he admits that he knows perfectly well what time is, as long as nobody asks him. The moment he tries to explain it, he does not know. The past is gone. The future is not here yet. And the present has no length at all. It is already slipping into the past as you notice it. Yet we measure time, and spend it, and say we are running out of it. Sixteen hundred years later, his question is still ours. What is time?
