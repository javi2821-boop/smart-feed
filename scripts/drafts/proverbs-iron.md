title: Iron sharpens iron
character: pastor

source: Proverbs 27:17 (KJV)
---
Proverbs twenty-seven, seventeen, in the King James, says this. Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend. Think about how that works. Two soft things cannot sharpen each other. And sharpening is not gentle. It is friction, and a little of the blade is lost each time. The proverb is saying that a real friend is not only someone who agrees with you and makes you feel good. It is someone hard enough to push back, and close enough that you let them. Most of us have plenty of people who are pleasant to us. Who in your life is actually allowed to sharpen you?
