type: lesson
title: Start with your time
character: businessman
background: city-street
source: Peter Drucker, The Effective Executive (paraphrased)
---
Peter Drucker spent decades watching executives, and he noticed that the effective ones did not start with their tasks. They started with their time. He argued that you cannot manage what you have not seen, so the first step was to write down where the hours actually went, as they happened, not from memory. Memory flatters us. Almost everyone who tried it was surprised. The work they called most important got scraps, and things nobody needed ate whole afternoons. You do not need a new system this week. Just keep an honest log for three days, and then read it as if it belonged to someone else.
